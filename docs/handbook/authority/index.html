Authority

Required: every spec has this.

Authority states who decides each part of the game: you, the builder, or a question answered for each build.

02-mechanics.md, a prose chapter

The furnace processes one piece of ore at a time. Any additional ore
waits in a queue. A finished bar remains in the furnace until the player
collects it.

> DELEGATED: furnace-presentation
>
> Decide how the furnace looks and sounds while processing ore. A player
> crossing the room must be able to tell that it is still running.

> PERSONALIZATION: furnace_signal
>
> Choose how the furnace signals that a bar is ready: a steady glow, or
> a glow with a soft chime. Either choice must be clear from across the
> room.

The first paragraph has no tag, so it is Fixed. The builder follows it as written.

  • Delegated asks the builder to decide within the limits you provide.
  • Personalization connects the prose to a declared question. Each build records one answer.

The label after DELEGATED: is optional free text. It describes the choice for readers and does not have to match a declared id. The id after PERSONALIZATION: is different: furnace_signal must be declared as a question in personalization.json, or validation fails.

Each tag applies until the next authority tag or the end of the section. A section ends at the next heading of the same or a higher level. The tag's limits still apply: the builder can choose the running animation, but it must remain readable from across the room.

The Personalization example changes presentation. Personalization can also offer gameplay alternatives that you define. A Delegated choice may not change how the game plays.

The personalization chapter explains how to declare the question and its answers.

Some games swap one complete set of active rules for another during play. > RULESET: <id> limits a section to one such ruleset, and exactly one tag adds (initial) to name the starting set.

02-mechanics.md, changing the gate rules between two acts

### Act one
> RULESET: act-one (initial)

The east gate opens after the bell puzzle.

### Act two
> RULESET: act-two

The east gate begins open and cannot close.

Both tags declare their ruleset ids. The section under act-one applies at the start; the later section applies after the game changes to act-two.

Full rules: OpenGDD specification, §2, §2a, and §2c.
