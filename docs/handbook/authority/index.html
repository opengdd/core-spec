3. Authority

You control the creative boundaries of your game: what is settled, and what a builder is free to decide. One authority tag above a paragraph says which is which.

02-mechanics.md, a prose chapter

Ore feeds into a furnace one piece at a time. A furnace runs one job,
and anything else dropped in waits its turn. A finished bar sits in the
furnace until someone takes it.

> DELEGATED: how a running furnace and a finished one look and sound.
> A player crossing the room should be able to tell the two apart
> without stopping.

Prose without a tag is your explicit decision. The > DELEGATED: tag hands some direction to the builder: a blockquote line, followed by any label that helps a reader. It holds until the next tag or the end of the section.

  • Fixed is the default. Untagged prose is Fixed, and the builder changes nothing.
  • Delegated hands the stated area to the builder, to decide within the intent you wrote.
  • Personalization asks a question the builder can answer on their own; its machinery is the personalization chapter's subject.

Delegated is not abandoned. The intent around the tag still binds: a builder may choose the animation, but cannot leave a finished furnace hard to spot. Untagged needs no ceremony either. What you simply write is yours.
