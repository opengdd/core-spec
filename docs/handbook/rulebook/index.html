14. The rulebook

You have met every rule on this page already, each in the chapter that owns it. Here they are stated once, plainly, as the law the validator enforces line by line. Scan the page before you ship a package, and read it as a reference rather than as homework.

The package and manifest#

  • A package MUST carry manifest.json, tuning.json, 01-overview.md, 02-mechanics.md, and 05-build-plan.md. 03-content.md and 04-presentation.md are optional.
  • A chapter holding the content of one of those five roles MUST use that exact filename. Chapters of your own MAY be named however you like.
  • The manifest MUST declare opengdd, id, version, title, designer, target, and build.
  • Every path a package writes MUST stay inside the package once its .. segments resolve.

(The shape of a spec)

The fantasy block#

  • 01-overview.md MUST open with a fenced fantasy block, after the title and before anything else.
  • The block MUST hold at least one fantasy line, and the fantasy lines together MUST NOT exceed 280 characters.
  • The block MUST carry one Feel: line naming three to five adjectives.
  • The block MUST carry one anti-reference line, opening NOT: or Anti-references:, and that line MUST NOT be empty. Each of the two labels MUST appear at most once.
  • A fantasy line MUST NOT carry a citation of any kind: not a tuning key in backticks, not a typed reference wearing state:, content:, descriptor:, or the expression form tuning:, and not a chapter anchor such as 03-content.md#the-crop-catalog.

(The fantasy block)

Authority tags#

  • Prose with no tag above it is Fixed, and a builder MUST build it as written.
  • A tag holds from where it sits to the next tag in that heading section, or to the end of the section.
  • Every difference a player could notice that changes how the game plays MUST be settled: written out as Fixed, or handed over on purpose.
  • A > PERSONALIZATION: tag MUST name a question declared in personalization.json.

(Authority)

Tuning#

  • Every runtime number a rebalance may change MUST live under tunables. A number the game's identity rests on lives under constants, and a rebalance MUST NOT touch it.
  • Only numbers live in tuning.json, and every one MUST be finite.
  • A tuning key MUST be two or more segments joined by dots. A bare word is not a key.
  • A key MUST sit in exactly one of tunables and constants, and every meta key MUST name a key that is there.
  • A range is legal only on a tunable, and the value in the file MUST sit inside it.
  • Prose that settles a rule MUST cite the tuning key, never the number stored under it.

(Tuning, and Identifiers)

Collections and graphs#

  • A collection MUST declare all six of id, format, defined_in, source, authority, and id_field. A one-file catalog is no exception.
  • Every declared file and entry MUST exist, and every stable id MUST be unique inside its collection.
  • An edge site MUST name from.collection, field, and to, and every id in that field MUST resolve to an entry in a permitted collection.
  • A claim about the shape of a graph, such as never looping back, MUST be made by a document-check acceptance test. Declaring the edges claims nothing beyond resolution.

(Collections, and Graphs)

Modes and clocks#

  • Every declared clock MUST state exactly one behavior for every declared mode.
  • A behavior MUST be one of advances, frozen, discrete-only, and does-not-exist.
  • Exactly one mode is active at a time, and modes MUST NOT nest.

(Modes & states)

Acceptance tests#

  • The build plan MUST carry at least one acceptance test, numbered from AT-1, unique and ascending in document order. A retired number is never reused.
  • Every AT-n heading MUST be followed by a fenced test block, and then the same check in one plain sentence.
  • Every test block MUST declare a type: scenario, property, exhaustive-search, or document-check.
  • The format's own test-block field names are closed, and a field of your own MAY sit beside them.

(Acceptance tests)

Direction and palette#

  • A direction block in 04-presentation.md and a declared direction.json MUST appear together. Either one without the other fails validation.
  • Every judged entry, meaning a pillar, a mood, an anti-reference, and a must_keep, MUST name one declared viewing context.
  • Every must_keep entry MUST list at least one may_vary axis.
  • Every mood MUST carry an intent and at least one anti entry, and every reference MUST name at least one property it borrows.
  • A palette role MUST carry value, tolerance, and scope. must_match: true is legal only at tolerance: 0.
  • Every entry under constraints MUST be cited by at least one acceptance test. A mood palette role MAY go uncited, and is then judged with its mood.

(Direction, and Palette & pinning)

Personalization#

  • Every question MUST carry an id, a prompt, a type of choice, text, or number, and a default.
  • A choice question MUST declare its options, and its default and every recorded answer MUST name one of them.
  • A key an answer overrides or resolves into MUST be a tunable. A constant is never legal there.
  • Every resolved value MUST land inside its key's declared range, and every path in affects MUST exist.

(Personalization)
