11. Direction: pillars, mood, must_keep

The look and feel of your game is yours to direct, and the format gives that direction a file of its own. In direction.json you state the visual and emotional intent every build answers to. A short fenced direction block sits at the top of 04-presentation.md, and the two travel together: neither is legal without the other.

direction.json, beside your manifest

{
  "pillars": {
    "white-on-white-still-reads": {
      "statement": "Snow reads against snow: depth comes from shadow and edge, never from tinting the ground another color.",
      "viewing": "climb-camera",
      "tie_break_order": 1
    },
    …
  },
  "must_keep": {
    "the-rope-stays-legible": {
      "statement": "The rope between climbers stays visible in every weather, and its slack reads at a glance.",
      "may_vary": [
        "rope color and material",
        "how falling snow is rendered",
        "HUD styling around the weather warning"
      ],
      "viewing": "climb-camera"
    }
  },
  …
}

Three constructs carry a direction:

  • A pillar is one statement every frame answers to; tie_break_order says which pillar wins when two of them pull apart.
  • A mood names the feel: a descriptor in your manifest, built from references to real works, with an intent every mood states and an anti list of what it is not.
  • must_keep states what survives every build, and may_vary lists the freedom around it; that list is never empty.

Which file holds what is a rule: every claim a build is judged or measured against lives in direction.json, and the mood descriptors those claims point at, with their references and their palettes, live in manifest.json.

Write the anti list generously. Naming what the mood is not is the surest way to hold a build away from the genre's default look.

These entries are judged by people rather than measured by a machine. A panel watches the finished game under the viewing context the entry names, here climb-camera, and reads your statements against what they see. You declare each viewing context once, in the viewing block of direction.json, and every judged entry names one. Most direction belongs in that tier.

A reference binds only what its borrows line names, the way a mood board cites Shadow of the Colossus or Kentucky Route Zero for one quality each: silhouette weight, how light falls. The rest stays open.

Notice the fragment carries no colors. Exact color values, and the tolerance a machine measures them by, are the palette and pinning chapter's subject.
