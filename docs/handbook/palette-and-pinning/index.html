12. Palette & pinning

A game's palette is identity, the way you know a frame of Journey or Limbo with the sound off. A palette role writes one of your colors down where the builder reads it: the color, how close a build must stay to it, and what wears it. You define the colors once, and every build follows them instead of guessing your look from adjectives.

manifest.json, inside a mood descriptor

{
  "id": "quiet-dark",
  …
  "palette": {
    "light-flame": {
      "value": "#E8A13C",
      "tolerance": 14,
      "scope": {
        "applies_to": "candle flame cores and the lantern the player carries",
        "states": ["in-play"],
        "sampling": { "sampled": { "verdict": "per-sample" } }
      }
    }
  }
}

Three fields carry it. value is the color, six hex digits in the form every color picker exports. tolerance is how far a build's color may drift, measured as perceptual color distance: 0 means identical, 1 is the smallest difference an eye can catch, and the 14 above keeps builds within nearby shades of the same flame. scope says what wears the color and when. The colors travel with the spec, and a builder on the other side of the world lights the same flame.

How precise to be is your choice, color by color. The format has names for the three levels:

  • described: prose, no measurement. Always available, and most direction stays here.
  • bounded: a color with a tolerance, like the 14 above. A build stays inside it.
  • exact: tolerance: 0. Every build reproduces the six digits.

None of the three words appears in your files. They name how precisely you wrote a color down, and the entry's own shape is what places it.

A color in a mood is read by people: the reviewers who score a finished build against your mood, the direction chapter's judged tier. Want a machine to hold a color instead? Cite the role from an acceptance test by its path, descriptors.mood.quiet-dark.palette.light-flame, and the test checks captured frames against the tolerance the role already declares. At the far end, must_match: true on an exact role makes a certified pin: spend it on the color that is the game. Exact colors, contrast thresholds, and timings can also live under constraints in direction.json, the checked tier of the direction chapter.

Distribution and harmony stay open. Roles say which colors, never how much of each or where the light falls; that balance stays the builder's craft, judged against your mood. Nothing forces a color to any level, and a color left to the reviewers is a fine place to stay.
